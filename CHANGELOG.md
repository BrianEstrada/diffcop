### Features

- **action:** Move a review's stats into a collapsed Run details section ([#51](https://github.com/BrianEstrada/diffcop/pull/51))

- **review:** Collapse every section and put the counts in the headline ([#58](https://github.com/BrianEstrada/diffcop/pull/58))
- **action:** Decide what to review from the last review, not from the event ([#59](https://github.com/BrianEstrada/diffcop/pull/59))
